UDP loopback through the board, 2026-09-28
host/udp_loopback_viewer.py sends RGB24 test video from the host (ConnectX-4, enp2s0np0,
192.168.100.2) to the board (eth0, 192.168.100.1), the board sends every packet back, and the
host reassembles each frame and compares it byte by byte with what it sent.
"intact" = every packet of the frame came back and the frame is identical; "incomplete" = at
least one packet missing. Rates are payload per direction. Driver: patches/0001 + 0002 (B).
Each block lists the settings in force; the tools improved between blocks.
